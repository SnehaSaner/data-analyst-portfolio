## 🎯 Business Objective
ToyJoy had ₹2.0M locked in excess inventory *and* frequent stockouts on its best sellers at the same time — a classic sign of reactive, non-data-driven inventory management. Across **5 product categories and 9,221 transactions**, this analysis was built to:
1. Optimize inventory allocation for capital efficiency
2. Maintain availability while cutting holding costs
3. Align replenishment with actual demand patterns

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | **₹4.23M** |
| Units in Inventory | **427K** |
| Stockout Rate | **4.65%** |
| Identified Opportunity Value | **₹675K (annual, achievable in 90 days)** |
| Overall Inventory Turnover | **42.68%** (vs. 50–60% industry benchmark) |

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Category turnover spread is huge:** Action Figures turn over at **50.19%** (best) while Board Games sit at just **12.21%** (worst) — capital is trapped in the wrong categories.
- **Reallocating 15% of inventory from low-turnover Puzzles to high-turnover Action Figures** is projected to lift overall inventory efficiency by **10%**.
- **Overstock hotspot:** the 1000pc Coral Reef Puzzle alone has **₹150K+ of capital blocked**; total overstock reduction could free up **₹500K+ in working capital** and cut holding costs 15–20% — the single largest near-term lever.
- **Stockout hotspot:** Ultra Robo Warrior (a top-5 revenue product) is chronically under-stocked, contributing to an estimated **₹200K in annual lost revenue**; fixing the top 5 stockout SKUs could recover **₹100K+**.
- **Seasonality is a major driver:** Holiday season alone accounts for **33.7%** of annual revenue and requires maximum safety stock, while Easter/Spring is a clearance opportunity, not a stocking priority.
- Top 5 products (Tycoon Strategy Game, Quest for Kingdom, 3D Solar System Puzzle, Ultra Robo Warrior, Baby Nurture Set) represent **35–40% of total revenue** and need differentiated, priority safety-stock treatment.

| 🟣 High | High demand – Tycoon Strategy Game | Increase safety stock 40%, prioritize supplier allocation | 30–60 days |
| 🟣 High | Seasonal mismatch – Board Games | Implement seasonal forecasting, adjust reorder points quarterly | 30–60 days |
| 🟡 Medium | Slow sales – Puzzles | Bundle with hero products, cross-sell | 60–90 days |
| 🟡 Medium | SKU proliferation – Board Games | Rationalize SKU count to top 3 performers | 60–90 days |

## 🧰 Methodology
9,221 transactions across supplier → warehouse → distribution were analyzed in Excel to compute category-level turnover, stockout frequency and cost, and overstock/capital-lock-up by SKU, then prioritized using an impact × urgency matrix.
