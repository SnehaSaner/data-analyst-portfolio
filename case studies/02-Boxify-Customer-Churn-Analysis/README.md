## 🎯 Business Objective
Boxify (online subscription box platform) was experiencing a critical surge in customer cancellations, most occurring **before the 6-month mark**. Leadership commissioned this analysis to:
1. Diagnose **why customers churn**
2. Identify **high-risk segments**
3. Quantify **revenue impact**
4. Design **targeted retention interventions**

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Active Subscribers | **17,448** |
| Churned Subscribers | **6,682** |
| Revenue Lost to Churn | **$6,325,246 (28.4% of potential revenue)** |
| Churn Share from Monthly Plans | **63%** |
| Projected Churn Reduction | **↓ 7%** over 2 quarters |

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Monthly (month-to-month) plans are the single biggest churn driver** — 446 of ~709 churned accounts, a 63% share — because they carry the lowest switching cost/commitment.
- **Low engagement + monthly plan = the largest churn segment** (338 customers) — engagement, not demographics, is the real predictor of churn.
- Churn is **fairly flat across age groups (44–51%)**, meaning age-based targeting is a weak lever; **behavioral segmentation works better than demographic segmentation.**
- **Ahmedabad (33.2%) and Bengaluru (26.9%)** show the highest churn shares — but the report flags this as secondary to behavior-based intervention.
- **High-risk customer profile:** monthly subscription, age 18–27, medium engagement, short tenure, lower cumulative spend — this profile should be flagged for retention outreach **before Month 3.**

## ✅ Recommendations

| Horizon | Action |
|---|---|
| **Quick Win** | Convert Monthly → Quarterly subscribers via a "Save 15% when upgrading" incentive |
| **Quick Win** | Send engagement reminders after 10–15 days of inactivity |
| **Quick Win** | Reward customers after their 3rd purchase (habit-formation window) |
| **Long-Term** | Referral discount program |
| **Long-Term** | Formal loyalty rewards program |
| **Long-Term** | Birthday / surprise box campaigns for retention |

**Projected impact:** implementing personalized retention campaigns, engagement nudges, and loyalty rewards is projected to reduce churn by **6–8%** over the next two quarters, improving customer lifetime value and renewal rates.

## 🧰 Methodology
Subscriber-level data (active + churned) was segmented by plan type, age, engagement level, and geography in Excel; churn rate and revenue-at-risk were calculated per segment to isolate the highest-leverage retention intervention.
