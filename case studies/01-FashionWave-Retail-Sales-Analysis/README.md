## 🎯 Business Objective

FashionWave wanted to know:

1. Can we grow **weekend sales by 15%** next quarter?
2. Which product categories are **underperforming on profitability**?
3. Are our **discounts protecting or destroying margin**?
4. What **data-driven actions** should leadership take next?

|-|-|
|Total Revenue|**₹4.75 Cr**|

## 🔍 Key Findings

* **Shoes** is the revenue and profit leader (₹1.21 Cr sales / ₹34.9L profit); **Beauty** has the highest margin but is significantly under-marketed relative to its profitability potential.
* **Weekend sales are structurally underutilized** — only 27.7% of revenue despite representing 2/7 of the week.
* **Discounts above 20%** produce sharply lower profitability without proportional sales growth — a clear elasticity ceiling.
* **Returning customers** are the most valuable segment and should anchor loyalty investment.
* Store performance varies widely by city: **Pune** and **Lucknow** lead, while **Hyderabad** lags significantly and needs an operational review.
* Bottom 5 products by revenue (T-shirt, Top, Belt, Kids Tee, Kurti) suffer from low differentiation and weak merchandising — not necessarily weak demand.

## ✅ Recommendations

**Quick Wins (0–30 days)**

* Launch weekend flash-sale campaigns
* Bundle Shoes + Accessories (top performer + high-margin add-on)
* Introduce loyalty rewards for returning customers
* Cap discounts at 20% enterprise-wide
* Targeted marketing lift for underperforming stores

**Long-Term Strategy (30–180 days)**

* Customer segmentation for personalized promotions
* Inventory allocation driven by sales-trend data
* Predictive demand forecasting
* Centralized KPI dashboard + periodic store performance reviews

## 🧰 Methodology

Sales transaction data (50 stores, 24 months) was cleaned and analyzed in Excel using pivot tables to break down revenue/profit by category, city, weekday/weekend, and discount band, then translated into an executive PowerPoint narrative.
