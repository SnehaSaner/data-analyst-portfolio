## 🎯 Business Objective
TastyBites' top-line revenue looked healthy, but leadership suspected margin was quietly eroding. This analysis of **972 orders** across product, customer, and payment dimensions was commissioned to:
1. Separate **margin champions from margin destroyers** across the SKU portfolio
2. Quantify how **discount depth** affects profitability
3. Compare **revenue vs. profit** to expose the gap between top-line and bottom-line
4. Recommend **pricing corrections**

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Revenue rank ≠ profit rank.** High sales don't automatically translate into high profitability — several top-revenue items are actually margin-weak.
- **Paneer Pizza — Profit Champion:** highest revenue *and* a strong 59.9% margin — the strongest contributor to overall profitability.
- **Margherita Pizza — Margin Pressure Product:** generates the *highest* revenue in the portfolio but only a 32.3% margin — a sign of aggressive, unmanaged discounting.
- **Cold Coffee — Hidden Profit Gem:** moderate revenue but the highest margin among major products (72.5%) — an underexposed asset.
- **Veg Biryani — High Volume, Low Value:** strong demand, but a 31.4% margin suggests high ingredient cost or underpricing.
- **Category view:** Meals is the core profit engine (₹3.0L revenue, 47% margin); Dessert has the *highest* margin (62%) but the lowest volume — a clear under-marketed category.
- **Returning customers generate 58% of revenue** — the most valuable segment by a wide margin.
- **Discount threshold identified:** profit margin declines sharply once discounts exceed **18–20%**, even though sales volume holds — meaning those discounts are pure margin giveaways.

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Increase prices 5–8%** on Paneer Pizza and White Sauce Pasta — high-demand, price-inelastic anchors that can absorb the change.
2. **Enforce a 15% discount cap** category-wide — the analytically validated profitability threshold.
3. **Replace uniform discount campaigns** with segment-specific offers, prioritizing returning-customer rewards over broad new-customer discounting.
4. Bundle Beverages and Desserts with Meals to lift AOV without sacrificing margin.
5. Promote "hidden gem" products (Chicken Wrap, Veg Biryani, French Fries) more aggressively at healthy margins.

**Projected impact (next 2 quarters):** Revenue ₹4.33L → **₹4.65L**, Profit ₹2.02L → **₹2.45L**.

## 🧰 Methodology
Order-level data (972 orders) was analyzed in Excel to compute per-product and per-category revenue vs. profit, discount-vs-margin sensitivity, and customer/payment-method breakdowns, then synthesized into pricing recommendations.
