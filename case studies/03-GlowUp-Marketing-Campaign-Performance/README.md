## 🎯 Business Objective
GlowUp needed to know where its ₹19.85M marketing budget was actually working — across **Social Media, Email, and Influencer Marketing** — and how to reallocate spend for maximum ROI. 1,500 campaign records were evaluated across a full fiscal year.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Marketing Spend | **₹19.85M** |
| Revenue Generated | **₹27.46M** |
| Overall Portfolio ROI | **38.33%** |
| Leads Generated | **1.87M** |
| Conversions | **197K** |
| Average CPA | **₹190** |

## 🔍 Key Findings

1. **Social Media is the revenue backbone** — 67.83% of total revenue at 27.93% ROI. High reach, but targeting/creative optimization is leaving ROI on the table.
2. **Influencer Marketing is the hidden growth lever** — only 5.84% of spend, yet the **highest ROI at 131% / 73.97% by channel-level calc** and lowest CPA (₹74–89) — it is structurally underfunded relative to its efficiency.
3. **Email Marketing is structurally broken** — negative ROI (‑1.9% to ‑16.5% depending on calc basis) and the highest CPA in the portfolio; this channel needs a strategy overhaul, not just a spend cut.
4. **Engagement ≠ Revenue** — campaigns like "Student" drove strong reach (31% ROI, high traffic) but converted inefficiently, pointing to funnel leakage rather than a targeting problem.
5. **Budget allocation is historically biased, not performance-driven** — current spend doesn't track channel efficiency.
6. **Campaign tiering:** "Summer Glow" (92.25% ROI) and "Luxury" (79.54% ROI) are portfolio anchors worth scaling; **"Winter Glow" is the critical outlier** — highest spend among underperformers, delivering a ‑17.25% ROI.
7. **Funnel diagnostics:** CTR 5.28% (strong reach, low engagement) → Lead Rate 6.67% (landing page optimization needed) → Conversion 10.51% (healthy sales efficiency).

## ✅ Recommendations

**Immediate (0–30 days)**
- Scale Influencer budget by 20–30%
- Cut Winter Glow budget by 40–50%
- Fix conversion-funnel leaks (landing pages, creative)

**Strategic Roadmap (30–180 days)**
- Expand Summer Glow & Luxury campaign formats
- Continuous performance-based budget reallocation
- Full revamp of Email Marketing strategy

**Quantified opportunity:** shifting 10–15% of Email budget into Influencer Marketing is projected to generate **₹400K–₹600K in incremental annual revenue** based on current channel efficiency ratios.

## 🧰 Methodology
1,500 campaign-level records were analyzed in Excel across spend, revenue, leads, and conversions by channel and campaign; ROI, CPA, and revenue-to-spend ratios were computed to rank channels and campaigns, then translated into a reallocation plan.
